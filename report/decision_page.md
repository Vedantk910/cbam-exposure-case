# Decision page: CBAM Exposure Case

**Question.** A 600 t/yr Indian fastener maker asks what to do about CBAM before 2034.

**Answer.** Buy verified rod from your current mill and verify your own plant. Do not wait, do not buy green steel yet, and do not leave the EU.

| Position | Present-value hit, 2027-34 |
|---|---|
| Verified rod, current mill | EUR 23k |
| Low-carbon rod | EUR 417k |
| Verify own plant only | EUR 879k |
| Stay on defaults | EUR 1.06M |
| Sell outside the EU | EUR 1.31M |

Hit is what the exporter absorbs after a Taiwanese rival's own default-route bill, plus fixed costs, at a flat EUR 82.32/t and 12%.

## Why

1. **The bill is real now.** The "2.5% in 2026" shorthand holds only at the benchmark. Certificates are `embedded emissions - free allocation`, and free allocation is a benchmark amount. India's marked-up default for bolts is 4.6x that benchmark, so 79% of embedded emissions is charged in 2026, not 2.5%. That is 4.96 certificates a tonne against 0.16, a factor of 32.
2. **The cost is large against the price.** On defaults, a tonne of bolts carries EUR 458 in 2027 (17% of the 2025 EU import price), rising to EUR 612 by 2034 (23%).
3. **The money is in the rod.** Verifying the plant alone saves EUR 183k. Adding verified rod saves EUR 1.04M. 73% of the prize comes from the mill's report.
4. **Green steel loses for now.** Free allocation falls with the cleaner route, which takes back 77% of the gain in 2027.

## Triggers

- Verified rod beats defaults above **55 t/yr** of EU sales. Verifying the plant alone needs 217 t/yr.
- Any verified rod below **5.55 tCO2e/t** beats the marked-up default from 2028.
- Low-carbon rod beats verified rod if the mill is above **3.9 tCO2e/t**, or if carbon passes **EUR 112/t** in 2034 at a EUR 179/t premium.

## Sector

India's in-scope exports to the EU were EUR 5.29bn in 2025 (4.32 Mt). On defaults that is EUR 1.16bn in 2026, 22% of value, against EUR 41M on the shorthand. Outside estimates are lower: FEPS-NIPFP 16% of 2022 unit value, Sentra.world 10-20% of product cost. Commodity grades carry the most, at up to 44% of value in 2026.

## Diverting

The EU took 37% of India's 2025 bolt tonnes. Rest-of-world prices are about EUR 391/t lower. One firm can move. The sector cannot, with the US under Section 232 and the UK starting its own CBAM in January 2027.

## Trade so far

Through 2026-08, the average effect of the default burden on EU imports is not distinguishable from zero (beta -0.34, se 0.34; placebo t -0.2). A stricter version gives -0.93 (t -2.3), but its pre-2026 points are not flat, so it is not relied on. Certificates are not on sale until February 2027.

## Honest limits

- No exporter, mill or verifier was interviewed. The exporter is fictional.
- No Indian mill's verified figure is public. 2.6 tCO2e/t is an estimate and the largest risk.
- Verification cost is a press figure at its upper end. Treble it and the trigger is 164 t/yr.
- Coverage of 7318 and 7326 by the steel quotas is inferred; Annex I of Reg. 2026/1384 was not read.
- Carbon is flat at the latest quarterly price, not a forecast.
