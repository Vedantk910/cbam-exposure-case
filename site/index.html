Executive summary.

Situation

EU importers have owed CBAM on steel and aluminium goods since January 2026. India sold of those goods to the EU in 2025.

Complication

The 2.5% people quote only holds at the EU benchmark. On default values Indian bolts are charged on of their carbon, and the mark-up rises to 30% in 2028.

Question

Should an exporter stay on default values, verify its own plant, change its steel, or leave the EU?

Answer

Buy verified rod from the current mill. That saves over eight years, pays above a year, and needs no capital spending. Low-carbon rod is the next step, to be tested again around 2030.

Implementation roadmap.

Four moves, tied to dates the rules already set.

  1. Now to Jan 2027

    Secure the data

    Ask the rod mill for its CBAM verification report on 2026 production, start logging fuel and output by product, and line up a verifier. The first visit has to be physical.

    Owner: managing director and procurement
  2. Feb to Sep 2027

    Agree who keeps the saving

    Give buyers verified data before the first declaration, and write the split into contracts. The importer pays CBAM and the exporter pays for verification.

    Owner: sales and finance
  3. 2028

    Buy as a cluster

    The default mark-up reaches 30% and verified data can move through the registry. One small exporter has little pull on a mill, while a cluster's rod order can ask for the report and share a verifier.

    Owner: industry association
  4. Around 2030

    Test low-carbon rod again

    Switch if carbon heads for /t by 2034, if the premium falls, or if your mill's verified figure comes in above tCO₂e/t.

    Owner: managing director

Risks to the recommendation.

RiskWhy it mattersResponse
The mill cannot or will not share a verified figureThe recommendation depends on it, and small re-rolling mills are the least likely to have one.Ask early, buy as a cluster, and switch to a plant that holds a report.
Verifiers run out of capacityEurope's fastener distributors expect a bottleneck, so verified data could slip.Book now and stay on defaults for affected lots. If verified data slips to 2029 the ranking holds.
The buyer keeps the whole savingThe importer pays CBAM, so the saving lands with the buyer first.Agree the split in the contract before the first declaration.
The rules moveA slower phase-out is proposed, benchmarks are due for an update, and the fastener boundary is disputed.Review yearly. The ranking survives the proposal and the boundary reading.
Steel quotas reach downstream goodsThe Commission must review downstream products by June 2027, and a 50% duty would outweigh CBAM.Watch the review, and keep other markets warm.
